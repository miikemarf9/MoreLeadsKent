Privacy notice.

How we handle details you send to MoreLeads Kent.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

MoreLeads Kent is an independent web design and digital services business based in East Kent, England. This notice describes how we use personal information when you visit this website or contact us about our services.

Who is responsible for your information?

MoreLeads Kent is responsible for personal information supplied to us through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email MichaelMarfurt@outlook.com or use the contact details on our contact page.

What information do we collect?

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number (if supplied), business or website details, and the text of your message. Please don't send sensitive personal information in the enquiry form.

Why do we use it?

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss requirements, provide quotes and manage any follow-up you request. The legal basis will normally be our legitimate interests in handling business enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We do not sell your enquiry details to advertisers.

Who handles or receives the data?

Our form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service that delivers messages to our email inbox. The website's hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and access logs to deliver and secure the site. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which can involve requests to Google's servers. Providers may process information outside the UK; their applicable privacy terms and safeguards should be checked directly.

How long do we keep it?

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage follow-up or keep records required for business, accounting or legal purposes. You can ask us to review or delete your details, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us at the email above. You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates

We may update this notice when our services or processing change. The date above shows the latest version.

Booking software: This notice covers MoreLeads Kent's marketing website and enquiry form. Grab&Book has its own separate privacy practices for its software and users.
