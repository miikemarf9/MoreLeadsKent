How can the prices be so low?

MoreLeads Kent is an independent, lean operation, with straightforward starter packages rather than a large agency overhead. These prices apply to the package scopes shown — more complex requirements are quoted separately.

What's included in the £20/month?

Ongoing hosting and routine support within an agreed scope. We'll confirm the number and type of revisions, the domain arrangements and any exclusions in writing before you go ahead.

Prices are starting prices for the listed website packages, not for custom booking software. Domain registration, ownership, cancellation terms and additional work should be confirmed in your written quote before purchase. No minimum-term or ranking guarantees are implied here.

07 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions worth asking.

Will I be able to see the website before it goes live?+

Yes. We'll agree a review stage before launch so you can give feedback on the pages we're building.

Do I need to understand hosting or domains?+

No. We'll explain how your domain and hosting are managed and who owns them before any launch.

Can I use Grab&Book with my website?+

Yes, depending on your website and requirements. Grab&Book is our separate booking and customer-management product. It is not included in the £75 or £120 website packages; we'll explain software pricing, setup and any integration costs before you commit.

Who owns my website and domain?+

We will explain domain ownership, hosting access and what happens if you decide to move your site before you agree to the service. You should have these arrangements in writing.

What if I need more than the standard package?+

Tell us what you're after. Larger sites, special integrations and custom functionality can be scoped and quoted separately.
